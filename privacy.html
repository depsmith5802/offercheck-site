OfferCheck Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

OfferCheck works on your iPhone. It has no accounts and no servers, and it doesn't send your data anywhere. This page says what the app handles and where it stays.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party code that collects data.
  • OfferCheck never records your screen. It reads one screenshot when you tap, on your phone, then discards it.
  • Your rules, your shift log and your location points stay on your phone. We never receive them.

What OfferCheck reads

When you tap your button, a Shortcut you set up takes one screenshot of the offer on your screen and hands it to OfferCheck. The app reads the pay, miles, time and a few labels from it using Apple's on-device text recognition, works out Take, Pass or Review, and discards the screenshot. The screenshot is not saved to your photos or to the app's storage, not uploaded, and its text is not logged.

What OfferCheck keeps, on your phone only

  • Your rules (minimums per order, per mile and per hour, gas, drive-back settings).
  • A log of your shifts: the numbers read from each offer (pay, miles, minutes, stops), the answer the app gave and why, which offers you marked as taken, your earnings and gas fill-ups. It also keeps the name of the store beside each tip on a delivery screen, because that is how the tip is matched to an order. It does not keep customer names, addresses or the screenshot.
  • Totals worked out from your location during a shift (miles driven, drive-back time). No coordinates are kept.

Deleting a shift in the app removes it. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Location

Location is optional. If you allow it, OfferCheck uses your precise location only while a shift you started is running. It uses it to measure your real miles and the drive back to your zone. When your shift is finished in the app, it works out the totals and deletes the location points. If you end a shift with a Shortcut, the app finishes it the next time you open it, so you can add your total, and in any case on the first launch more than 12 hours after the shift ended. Until then the points stay on your phone. The points never leave your phone, are not synced by the app, and are never used for tracking or advertising. You can turn location off at any time in Settings. OfferCheck then falls back to a time limit and the miles you type in.

Because the points are saved on your phone while a shift runs, a device backup taken during a shift can include them. They are gone from the next backup after you end the shift.

Summaries

If your iPhone supports Apple Intelligence, the shift summary wording is written by Apple's on-device model. It runs on your phone.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. OfferCheck never sees your payment details. See Apple's terms and privacy policy for how Apple handles purchases.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share your data, because we don't have it.

Children

OfferCheck is for delivery drivers and isn't directed at children.

Independent app

OfferCheck is independent. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by DoorDash. It only advises: it never accepts, declines or taps anything in another app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new version is posted here.
