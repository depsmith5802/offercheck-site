OfferCheck Support

Questions, problems or ideas: offercheckapp@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model and iOS version. Please don't send screenshots with customer names or addresses.

Getting started

Open OfferCheck, set your rules, add the two Shortcuts with the buttons in the app, put a button on your screen, then tap it when an offer is showing. The Help tab in the app walks through each step.

It says it couldn't read the offer

OfferCheck needs an offer screen that shows the pay, the distance and the time. If one is missing, or your screen looks different from the sample offer in the app, it says so and doesn't guess. Compare your screen with the sample. It has only been tested with one delivery app.

Location questions

Location is optional and used only during a shift you started. Turn it off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > OfferCheck. Without it the app uses a time limit and the miles you type in.

Manage or cancel your subscription

On your iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > OfferCheck > Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period to avoid the next charge. To get your subscription back on a new phone, use Restore Purchases in the app.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Everything stays on your phone. See the Privacy Policy. Deleting the app deletes its data.

Independent app

OfferCheck is independent. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by DoorDash. It never accepts, declines or taps anything for you.
